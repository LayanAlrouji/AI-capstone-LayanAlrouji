# Workplace AI Implementation

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates the practical use of Artificial Intelligence in a workplace task: writing a professional job application email.

The project applies a structured R.A.C.E. prompting approach to generate, review, and refine an AI-assisted email while maintaining data privacy and verifying the final output.

## Objective

The objective is to demonstrate how AI can support a recurring text based workplace task by reducing drafting effort while keeping human review and decision making as essential steps.

## AI Tool Used

- Gemini

## Project Task

The selected task is writing a professional email when applying for job opportunities.

The email is designed to:
- Introduce the applicant.
- Express interest in the position.
- Refer to the attached CV.
- Maintain a concise and professional tone.

## R.A.C.E. Prompting

The project uses the four pillars of the R.A.C.E. framework:

- **Role:** Defines the role the AI should take.
- **Action:** Specifies the task the AI should perform.
- **Context:** Provides the relevant information about the job application.
- **Expectation:** Defines the required format, tone, length, and content.

## Iterative Refinement

The initial AI-generated email was reviewed and refined through follow-up prompts.

Two refinements were performed:
1. The Portfolio reference was removed because the job advertisement only requested a CV.
2. The email was expanded to provide more detail while maintaining a natural and professional tone.

## Data Masking

Sensitive information from the job advertisement was anonymised before using it with the AI tool.

Examples include:
- Company name → Company A
- Location → City1
- Email address → Masked

No personal names, account IDs, or confidential organisational information were included in the AI prompt.

## Verification

The final AI output was reviewed using the three-step verification approach:

1. **Read it as a sceptic** – Checked the content for unexpected or unsupported information.
2. **Check the hard parts** – Verified the job title, job type, location, and application requirement.
3. **Own it** – Reviewed the final email and decided that I would defend every line as my own.

No numerical data was used, so an arithmetic check was not applicable.

## Impact

- **Estimated time saved:** Approximately 10 minutes per job application email.
- **Quality improvement:** AI helped produce a clear, structured, and professional email that matched the job advertisement requirements.
